# HaulMatch 360 initial live SEO scope

## Published
Vehicle transport, car transport, bakkie transport, non-running vehicle transport, tractor transport, large farm implement transport, agricultural machinery transport, heavy equipment transport, lowbed transport, and vehicle towing/recovery.

## Deliberately not published
Livestock, pallets, furniture, household removals, courier loads, food delivery, refrigerated freight and general freight. These categories remain future expansion and are excluded from navigation, SEO pages and the sitemap.
